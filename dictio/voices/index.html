You vs.

How it works: every number is computed by the same small script for you and for them (sentence split on . ? !, a vowel-group syllable estimate, word lists for hedges and pronouns). Adjective and adverb dials come from the spaCy en_core_web_sm part-of-speech tagger run offline on each corpus; your text is measured in the browser, so it gets an "-ly words" count instead. Corpora are small (about 2,000 to 13,600 words each), mostly prepared speeches or printed text, and say more about a few documents than about a whole person.
